# Code review, 2026-10-31

A full read-only review of `Sources/`, `Plugins/`, `Tests/`, `scripts/` and `.claude/skills/run-peeker/`, at the level of files and types. Priorities, in order: single responsibility and KISS, then reliability, clarity and style. Five reviewers each took one area, and line numbers refer to commit `5d0d5f0`.

Status values: **open**, **in progress**, **fixed** (with the commit), **won't fix** (with the reason).

## Verdict

There's no deep structural problem. The plugin contract is coherent and the safe-write path is sound. Most file boundaries earn their place. The single-responsibility and KISS costs sit in four files that grew fast: `PDFPluginDocument`, `ImageDocument`, `AppDelegate` and `DocumentViewController`. The review also found about fifteen bugs, almost all small.

## What holds up

- The plugin contract is coherent: each recent member has a design record.
- `FileReplacement` is sound.
- PDF folding, the numbered pending list, `PageStash`, `PeekerPDFDocument.order` and `MovedPageRepair` all earn their place.
- The split between `EditController`, `FormFields` and `TextBoxEditor` is clean.
- `TextDocument` is coherent.
- Every new image file earns its own file.
- `drive.swift` has one job and stays under the split size.
- The isolation in `PeekerPDFDocument` and the frame callback in the image animation are sound and covered by tests.
